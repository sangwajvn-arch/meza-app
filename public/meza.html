Three steps, no app to download

Everything a diner needs is behind the code already on the table.

1

Scan the table code

Opens straight to that table's menu in the browser — in English, Kinyarwanda or French.

2

Build the order

Browse by category, adjust quantities, send it straight to the kitchen queue.

3

Pay by mobile money

MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or Urubuto Pay. Paste the confirmation SMS as proof — staff confirm it before the order is marked paid.

Built for the whole venue, not just the menu

One dashboard for the people actually running service.

Live kitchen board

Approved orders land on a kitchen screen the moment they're sent, sorted by table — nothing relayed by shouting across the pass.

Cashier & payment proof

Every mobile money SMS a diner submits is checked against the order total before it's accepted.

Stock & recipes

Each dish draws down its ingredients automatically, so low stock shows up before you run out mid-service.

Reports you can act on

Revenue, food cost, waste from voided orders, and net profit for any date range — exportable as a spreadsheet, with staff accounts kept separate by PIN.
